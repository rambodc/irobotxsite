Twenty years of looking forward.

iRobotX is a multidisciplinary technology company founded in 2006 and operating from Calgary, Alberta. We explore, design and develop systems for industries in motion.

Our perspective

Range is our advantage.

Breakthroughs rarely stay inside one discipline. A robotics problem can demand advances in perception. An industrial platform can benefit from consumer-grade experience design. A payment network can require the resilience of critical infrastructure.

iRobotX was built around that overlap. Our work has moved through oil and gas, enterprise software, humanoid robotics, digital finance and music and digital art—each field adding new knowledge to the next.

We bring a practical builder’s mindset to emerging technology: stay curious, understand the environment, prototype intelligently and engineer for real use.

The journey

Built through continuous evolution.

Our story is not a straight line through one sector. It is a growing body of technical experience connected by a commitment to ambitious work.
