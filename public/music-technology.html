A new digital canvas for music.

iRobotX is exploring platform technology that brings artists, music and digital art into a more connected creative economy—one where ownership, rights and participation can be reimagined.

Creative technology

Art is evolving beyond the format.

Music has always created culture. Digital technology now creates new ways for that culture to be expressed, shared, valued and owned.

iRobotX is developing a new platform concept around the music industry: a digital environment where artists can connect music with visual art, build richer relationships with audiences and explore new models for creative ownership.

Our focus is the infrastructure behind that future—identity, provenance, rights, participation and transparent digital value—designed around artists rather than technology alone.
