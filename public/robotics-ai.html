Intelligence with a physical purpose.

iRobotX explores the connected disciplines that bring humanoid systems to life—from visual perception and intelligent behaviour to the parts that make movement possible.

Embodied intelligence

Machines that can understand their world.

Humanoid robotics is not one technology. It is a demanding integration of perception, reasoning, mechanics, design and continuous adaptation.

Our experience spans AI for robotic vision, brain-inspired approaches to intelligent behaviour, humanoid component development and physical prototyping. We connect software intelligence with the realities of form, motion and fabrication.

The goal is not technology for spectacle. It is capable, understandable systems designed around real human environments.

Capability matrix

From perception to embodiment.

Each capability supports a complete development loop: model the problem, build the system, observe its behaviour and refine it.

01 / VISION

Machine perception

Computer vision concepts that help robotic systems interpret people, objects and changing environments.

02 / COGNITION

Brain-inspired AI

Exploration of intelligent architectures influenced by how biological systems process, associate and respond.

03 / HARDWARE

Humanoid components

Design thinking for the mechanical and integrated parts required by embodied robotic systems.

04 / FABRICATION

3D modelling & printing

Digital modelling and additive manufacturing for rapid physical iteration, fit testing and custom parts.
